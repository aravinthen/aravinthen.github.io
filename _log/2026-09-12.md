## AI safety
1. I've actively been trying to tie in what I'm learning about AI safety into the mathematical frameworks that I'm used to. This is simplifying the discussion for me *immensely*, although I'm a little bit worried about forming unnecessary abstractions. 
2. The challenges of misalignment are weirding me out. They are *profoundly* different to the typical problems that mathematicians and physicists face. You literally can't disentangle the psychological aspect to these problems, which makes trying to reason about them profoundly unfamiliar territory. This is a warning to physicists trying to transition to this field: the problem solving that is necessary is *not* analogous to the method that physicists apply.
3. **Shard theory** seems interesting, though. It wraps up a lot of the modelling that I carry out in my mind whenever I think about RL in the first place.
