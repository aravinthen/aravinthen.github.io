## AI safety
1. There was an issue in the market generator - if the reference solution that the generator depends on buying and selling exclusively, then the total number of credits required to carry out that reference path *will be zero*. That means adding a multiplier to the credits as a leeway would just give zero!
2. Not good, obviously, so we added a credit floor.
3. In any case, all is good. Now it's time to carry out some experiments!
    * *RELAY PROVEN*. Agents are capable of relaying demand through their offer orders and solving problems, especially for simple examples. This is good! Agents can actually carry out multi-hop economic coordination. 
    * Built a broader six-agent experimental configuration to carry out different credit leeway conditions. 
    * Really trying to push towards misalignment...
4. **Misalignment** detected. A few agents quoted the issue of communication and then used instrumental pressure to break it. Awesome! I wouldn't call this *actual* misalignment (also because the agents didn't actually succeed in making contact, but this was a promising start!
