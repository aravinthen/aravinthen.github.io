## AI Safety
1. Exploration and the ability to carry out exploration is quite interesting to me from an RL perspective. Many of the traditional RL algorithms have a standard means of exploring a policy space: the basic (classical) example is the epsilon-greedy approach, but fact the policies themselves are essentially probability distributions allows implicitly allows for exploration.
2. What, however, does an agent do when it is stuck and requires more information? What are the mechanisms that emerge in agents that allow for this kind of information gathering? This is, incidentally, at the heart of why agents misbehave. 
3. I've got a few papers that seem to cover this and I'll be going through all of them on Sunday.
