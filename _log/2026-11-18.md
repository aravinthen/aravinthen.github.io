    * Sub-optimal debaters: if the debaters are stupid, they'll just provide nonsense!

### A description of debate games
1. The debate environment: the parameters of the debate that the designers cannot change, consisting of
    * A set of worlds and a prior distribution,
    * A set of questions and answers,
    * A mapping that measures the deviation of an answer from the truth about that world, 
    * Experiments that the judge can perform to learn whether the current world has a particular property, which itself is can be used to inform answers and questions. 
2. The design elements of the debate: parts of the game that can be changed,
    * The choice of question and legal answers,
    * Communication language,
    * Argumentation protocol,
    * Termination conditions,
    * Experiment selection policies
    * Debating incentives
3. Once the debate environment and the design of the debate have been clarified, the **debate** is formalized as consisting of a tuple (E, q, G), with E being debate environment, q being the question and G being the game. Formally, the game unfolds as a zero-sum extensive form game.
    1. The world is sampled from the distribution of worlds and shown to the debaters alongside 1 and 2. 
    2. The debaters simultaneously pick an answer, 
    3. The debaters alternate making arguments in the communication language, stopping with regards to a termination condition, 
    4. A single experiment is selected and it's result used as context for...
    5. The scores (or utilities) that the agents obtain based on the experiment,
    6. The answer of the debater with the highest score is the outcome of the debate game.
    7. The debate error is the deviation of the truth of the world with the outcome of D.
