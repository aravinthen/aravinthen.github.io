## AI Safety
1. Having done quite a lot of AI reading up to this point, very little of the first chapter was particularly new to me. The *terminology*, however, was very useful. For example, *jaggedness* - the tendency of an AI to be superhuman at some tasks and less than human at others.
2. **Vending-bench** is nuts. I will say however that I'm getting a little... antsy about the results here. Yes, these results are really cool, but *what exactly changed to allow this new capability of business management to occur?*
3. Ah, here we go:
    * Scaling: _bigger models, more data_. The bitterest of lessons.
    * Reinforcement learning: my favorite. Less bitter. The newest term I've learned here is *constitutional AI*, which is essentially RLAIF. The problem here is how you train the AI judge
    * Recursive self-improvement. The technological singularity. I've been reading about this for years, now... can't believe I'm at a point where I might witness it.
4. I quite literally have no doubt in my mind that we'll be seeing some form of AGI in the next five years. Not after the recent mathematical results. Not after seeing AI build a 4D chess that could actually be played (?!). These may have started off as stochastic parrots, but any statistical physicist can tell you how noise suddenly collapses into coherent signals.
5. Given the question: **Really try to internalise what the trajectory of this technology means. What's one thing about this you find most exciting, and one thing you find most concerning?**
    * My answer: This is a genuinely superb example of exponential growth that leads into the ideas of the "technological singularity" that I was so enamored by as a teenager. In fact, the promise of the singularity was part of the reason I became interested in science in the first place. A world where intelligence was cheap, where the deepest scientific questions were available to be answered with little issue at all.
    * Nobody back then (about fifteen years ago) had any idea of what post-singularity humanity might look like. It felt so silly to me that, given this enormously consequential event, not a single person could provide a compelling argument as to what might happen after it! This is the real concern that we're currently now facing: the uncertainty as well as the infinite variance associated with this point.
    * As a summary, this technology can answer some of the greatest mysteries that mankind has ever pondered, and then guide thought on mysteries that mankind was once too limited to even consider. The concern is that we have no idea whether the technology will be used for this, and what it might be used for instead could be genuinely terrifying.
6. The foundational sociological reason that is presented in this course for why AI is bottlnecked by human attention is remarkably cognizant. This is a superb course!
7. "Some threats my be much harder to defend against than they are to create". Yup. I've thought about one such problem a *lot* (and I can't tell anybody about it). In general, there is always going to be an asymmetry associated with defense and attack.
8. ... uh. The Artificial Escalation video. What the hell was that? That is... oddly specific, isn't it? Just me?
9. How could one *technically* guard against an AI from gaining more power than is necessary? It would be an interesting experiment to see whether AI is given the choice for its tools and instead of using all possible tools it chooses a subset of them. Granted, I've never actually seen a situation like this in my own RL work...
