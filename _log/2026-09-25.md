## AI Safety 
1. I came across an incredibly interesting idea for alignment today, focused mostly on the discussion of *swarms* and methods of AI safety that require multiple agents coordinating in a game. 
2. One specific idea that jumped out to me is the use of a verifier-prover approach: this is where an agent generated a solution/reasoning trace whilst a verifier tests it for accuracy and correctness. This is a Stackleberg game and has some genuine mathematical guarantees that could be relevant for safety!
3. I also really like the idea of AI debate: a zero-sum game where two AI try and persuade a human of two sides of an argument. This could be a really, really good way of supporting scalable oversight and explainability.
