## AI Safety 
1. And that was the second discussion of the course. It was *fantastic*. Genuinely wonderful discussion. 
2. We spent an hour talking about AI alignment via swarms. One of the big insights I got from the discussion was that AI might *never* become aligned, or the actual alignment may be 99% airtight. This is bad enough for single agents, but the consequences that emerge for swarms are so dramatic that it might just damn the idea of ever safely being able to deploy a swarm in the first place...
3. Or not! As I mentioned in the meeting I feel confident that whilst agents may never reach full alignment, it may be possible to build societal structures that genuinely just don't permit agents to act in a misaligned way. Not just in a way where you force the agents to act in a particular way through guard rails, but in a way that the agents would have no reason to act poorly in the first place!
