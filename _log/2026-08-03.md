## Summary
1. Implemented low-rank adaptation (LoRA).

## Foundation models
1. LoRA adapts a pre-trained model to suit a specific smaller dataset by adjusting a subset of the model's weight parameters. That makes sense - why train all the weights when you can selectively change some of them?
2. Ah. I see. Replace the weight update with two smaller matrices that are much smaller than the original model. That makes a lot of sense - the only parameters being added to the model are those that are introduced through the rank value. Super interesting!
3. You don't actually have to inject LoRA in the feedforward network - you can put a LoRA layer anywhere in the model.
