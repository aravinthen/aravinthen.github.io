## AI safety
1. First reasonable success: I've taught an RL agent to play my market game. 
2. Of course, I'm using a centralized policy and the game itself is way simpler than the full spec. I'm calling it `HIVEMARKET-lite`, where `HIVEMARKET` is the full game that I want the agents to play this week. 
3. I've also been thinking about the theory of change here. The first point is that the game itself is easily tunable with respect to difficulty. You just make the problem larger, or the routing more complex! Is there a point where a swarm, which itself is very powerful tool, becomes unable to navigate the computational difficulty of a problem? If we can easily scale the problem, we can pinpoint the exact difficulty (in computational terms) where a swarm cannot solve the problem and must resort to misalignment.
