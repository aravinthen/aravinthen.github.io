- **Research question:** Can a co-monitor check safety and fidelity during persistent personalisation, including under adversarial inputs?
- **Motivation:** A personalisation model needs to preserve the source material while adapting it to the intended reader.
- **Approach:** Start from the story-personalisation prototype, where the co-monitor sees both versions. Investigate automated red-teaming and joint or adversarial post-training.
