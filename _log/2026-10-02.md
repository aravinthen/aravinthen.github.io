## AI Safety
1. Theoretical alignment is a more niche region within the full science of alignment. It is the mathematical field that concerns the fundamental questions and mechanisms of agent design. 
2. What is the scope of this as a discipline, though? Of course, the field of alignment focuses on aligning AI to human values, but human values are also engineerable. The target here isn't just "align to a particular standard", it is "align to a given standard as well as possible". 
3. The techniques that I looked at today are strong, but I see quite a lot of reliance on simpler models from game theory literature. I wonder how successful a lot of these techniques might transfer towards the ultra-complex machines that carry out inference right now?
