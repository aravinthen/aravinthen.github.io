## AI safety
1. The group that I'm part of (which we've taken to calling n-body-labs) is figuring out their basic mission statement, mostly for the purposes of clarifying recruitment. 
2. I am pretty heavily set on *building tools* for dealing with situations where swarms lose control. I'm personally not too concerned with understanding the finer points of behaviour, mostly because agents themselves change so rapidly that trying to study them is something of a moot point: they'll change themselves in a way that constantly counters any understanding that we have of them. 
3. This is scary. I spent a lot of time studying complex adaptive systems as a young PhD student, mostly because they were the kind of system that fascinated me as a soft matter physicist in the first place. However, that was as a scientist. What use is there developing a strong understanding of a machine that changes whenever you measure it? This is *scary*.
