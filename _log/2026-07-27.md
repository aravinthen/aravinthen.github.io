## Foundation models
1. Right, I know this much: RNN encoders generate a single hidden state, but they often lose context, or the hidden state itself isn't able to encode long-range dependencies: a compression issue. 
2. Recap time: *Bahdanau attention* - a means of incorporating all of the generated hidden states when carrying out inference. The hidden states are associated with a score in order to rank their relevance in the context.
3. Self-attention is merely the ability to apply attention to the input itself rather than the traditional usecases of attention, which relate the relevance of the input to an output.
4. "The goal of self-attention is to compute a context vector for each input element that combines information from all other elements". That's a really, really succinct and nice way of putting it. Context vectors are the goal of this calculation - they are *enriched* embeddings.
