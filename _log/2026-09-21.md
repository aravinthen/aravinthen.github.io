## Summary
1. Finished *Thinking in Systems: A Primer* by Donella Meadows. 

## Systems
1. This was a great book. It's so wonderfully general and does such an excellent job of drawing out similarities between highly disparate systems.
2. I have a strong feeling that I'm going to be appealing to systems concepts for a while now. I already sort of do, but in a way that is something of a mismash between thermodynamical language and generic complexity. What I'm somewhat worried about is conflating my understanding of AI safety in general with systems talk and ending up being incomprehensible due to jargon. That's really the essence of my discomfort with systems thinking: it is very, very jargon heavy and seems to encourage interdisciplinarity under it's *own* terms.
