## About

My name is Aravinthen Rajkumar. I'm currently employed as an algorithm engineer at MBDA UK, where I work on deep reinforcement learning algorithms.

I was a computational physicist in a previous life. I completed my PhD with the Centre of Doctoral Training in the Modelling of Heterogeneous Systems at the University of Warwick, where I specialised in computational statistical physics. My graduate work was based around developing advanced Monte Carlo algorithms for self-assembling soft matter systems and then predicting the physics of those systems using non-equilibrium molecular dynamics simulations. 

I spent six months deeply focused on reinforcement learning as a technique and a learning philosophy. I am now in the process of approaching frontier artificial intelligence, specifically in the fields of **post-training** and **reasoning**.
