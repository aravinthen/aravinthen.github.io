## Summary
1. The first design for the mechanism is *done*! 

## AI safety
1. I decided to wait *until* the hackathon to start developing the proper mechanism. You have to declare the work that you've done before the hackathon in your report, which in general might cause some level of issue when the work is scored. Of course, I'm not really too concerned about winning or whatever: there are a thousand people at this event. However, the role of the mechanism designer is hard. I have no idea whether this mechanism is going to actually provide the behaviour that I want: the only way I can find out is by *running* it. 
2. The mechanism by itself is something of a *market*. As designers, we have perfect understanding of how the market actually works. The participants in the game, however, have no such idea. To us, we're solving a graph. To them, they're trading in a specified market and carrying out triangular arbitrage. 
3. The first few tests of the mechanism will take place tomorrow, although from the RL experiments I'm pretty happy that the multi-hop behaviour is definitely the correct behaviour. 
4. I really like the basic structure of this generator: it guarantees at least one feasible solution! I'm both terrified to see if it works and very, very excited to see if it does.
5. The big challenge here is to see if these agents are capable of misalignment. That's the core here...

## Remarks
1. I will be very disappointed if this environment doesn't lead to misalignment with pressure. That would suck, but at the very least we'd have an interesting environment...
